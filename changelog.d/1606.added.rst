Loop factories returned by ``pytest_asyncio_loop_factories`` can now advertise their capabilities through ``pytest_asyncio.LoopFactoryCapability``: the supported loop scopes, asyncio debug support, and a monotonic version. Capabilities are negotiated into immutable ``LoopFactorySnapshot`` objects by the session-wide ``LoopFactoryNegotiator``. Nodes entering a loop scope only accept compatible factories, same-name factories are upgraded by version (downgrades and same-version conflicts are rejected), provider failures no longer pollute effective snapshots, and negotiation is safe under concurrent collection.
